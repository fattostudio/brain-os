# Manifesto

## This is not a portfolio. It's a place to look around.

Most portfolios are a corridor — you walk in one end, you're pushed past
the work in the order someone decided for you, and you leave out the other.
This one is a room instead. Things are scattered. Some are obvious, some
you'll only find if you start opening things you weren't told to open.

I make software for a living, and the way I think about software is the way
this place is built: a few strong objects, loosely arranged, that reward
the people who poke at them.

### What you'll find here

- Real projects, as folders you can open and rummage through.
- A description of each, in plain language, plus the working thing itself.
- Side projects and half-ideas, if you go looking.

### How to use it

Drag things. Open things. Close them. Move them out of the way. Nothing
here is precious and nothing is in a fixed place — the arrangement you
leave behind is yours.
